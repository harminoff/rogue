Rogue Archive notes:
  Super-Rogue was developed by a group of AT&T Bell Labs employees between
  1982 and 1983, with Robert D. Kindelberger as the public face of the group.
  Super-Rogue 9.0 is based on the Rogue 3.6 source code.

Distribution notes:
  The upstream archive includes LICENSE.TXT with BSD-style redistribution
  terms for source and binary distribution. Preserve LICENSE.TXT in source
  and packaged binary distributions.

Name-use restriction:
  The license says derived products may not be called "Super-Rogue" and may
  not use "Super-Rogue" in their product name without prior written permission.
  RogueTiles uses "Super-Rogue 9.0.1" only as a factual variant label.
